Basic Medical Care & Wellness Directory

Common Health Conditions (South Asian Focus)

PCOS & PCOD

Detailed Causes: Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) and Disease (PCOD) are deeply linked to hormonal imbalances (excess androgens), genetic predispositions, and low-grade inflammation. Insulin resistance plays a massive role, causing the pancreas to produce more insulin, which in turn spikes androgen production from the ovaries.

Comprehensive Dietary Guidelines:

What to Eat
  • High-fiber foods (oats, brown rice, broccoli, lentils).
  • Lean proteins (chicken, fish, tofu, legumes).
  • Anti-inflammatory healthy fats (walnuts, almonds, olive oil).
What NOT to Eat
  • Refined sugars (sweets, sodas, white breads).
  • Processed foods & saturated fats (fried items, chips).
  • Excess commercial dairy & processed meats.

Medical Management: For patients, treatments often involve hormonal birth control to regulate menstrual cycles, Metformin to improve insulin resistance, and anti-androgen medications (like Spironolactone) to reduce acne and excess hair growth. Always consult a gynecologist or endocrinologist before starting.

Thyroid Disorders

Detailed Causes: Thyroid disorders primarily include Hypothyroidism (underactive) and Hyperthyroidism (overactive). The most common causes are autoimmune diseases (Hashimoto's thyroiditis for hypo, Graves' disease for hyper).

Comprehensive Dietary Guidelines:

What to Eat
  • Iodine-rich foods (seaweed, fish, dairy, eggs).
  • Selenium sources (Brazil nuts, tuna, sardines).
  • Zinc-rich items (oysters, chicken, pumpkin seeds).
What NOT to Eat
  • Raw goitrogens (uncooked cabbage, broccoli, soy).
  • Highly processed foods.
  • Excessive refined sugar and artificial sweeteners.

Medical Management: Hypothyroidism is treated with synthetic thyroid hormone replacements (Levothyroxine). Hyperthyroidism treatments include anti-thyroid medications (Methimazole, Propylthiouracil).

Cardiac Arrest & Heart Health

Detailed Causes: Cardiac arrest is an electrical malfunction in the heart causing an irregular heartbeat (arrhythmia), leading the heart to suddenly stop pumping. Underlying causes include Coronary Artery Disease (CAD).

Comprehensive Dietary Guidelines:

What to Eat
  • Heart-healthy fats (olive oil, avocados).
  • Omega-3 rich fish (salmon, mackerel).
  • Leafy greens, berries, and whole grains (oats).
What NOT to Eat
  • Trans fats and hydrogenated oils.
  • Deep-fried foods and highly processed snacks.
  • Excessive sodium and sugary beverages.

Medical Management: Preventive care includes Statins (for cholesterol), Aspirin, ACE inhibitors, or Beta-blockers as prescribed by a cardiologist.

Diabetes (Sugar)

Detailed Causes: Diabetes mellitus is a metabolic disease causing high blood sugar. Type 2 (most common) involves insulin resistance—where the body cannot effectively use the insulin it produces.

Comprehensive Dietary Guidelines:

What to Eat
  • Low-GI foods (non-starchy vegetables, sweet potatoes).
  • Lean proteins (chicken, tofu, lentils).
  • High-fiber foods to slow glucose absorption.
What NOT to Eat
  • Refined carbohydrates (white rice, pasta, bread).
  • Sugary drinks (sodas, packaged juices).
  • Sweets, desserts, and large carb-heavy meals.

Medical Management: Type 1 requires lifelong insulin therapy. Type 2 is managed with lifestyle changes and medications like Metformin.

High & Low Blood Pressure

Detailed Causes: High BP is caused by chronic stress, high sodium, obesity. Low BP is caused by dehydration, severe infections, or nutritional deficiencies.

Comprehensive Dietary Guidelines:

What to Eat
  • The DASH diet: Bananas, spinach, berries (High BP).
  • Increase fluid and slightly increase salt (Low BP).
  • Potassium and Magnesium-rich foods.
What NOT to Eat
  • Excessive sodium (pickles, canned soups, processed meats).
  • Excessive caffeine and alcohol.
  • Ultra-processed junk food.

Dietary Minerals & Calcium Registry

Calcium (Ca)
Bones & Teeth

Why it's vital: Crucial for skeletal strength, muscle function, and nerve transmission. Essential to prevent osteoporosis, which is highly common in South Asian women.

Top Food Sources: Dairy (Milk, Paneer, Curd), Ragi (Finger Millet), Almonds, Sesame Seeds (Til), and Green Leafy Vegetables (Spinach, Methi).
Iron (Fe)
Oxygen Transport

Why it's vital: Needed to synthesize hemoglobin (which transports oxygen in blood). Extremely critical for South Asian women, where iron-deficiency anemia is highly prevalent.

Top Food Sources: Spinach, Lentils (Dals), Chickpeas, Beetroot, Pomegranate, Raisins, and Red Meat. Tip: Pair with Vitamin C (lemon juice) to enhance vegetarian iron absorption.
Magnesium (Mg)
Metabolic Support

Why it's vital: Controls over 300 biochemical reactions including muscle relaxation, nerve conduction, blood pressure, and blood glucose regulation.

Top Food Sources: Pumpkin Seeds, Almonds, Spinach, Bananas, Dark Chocolate, and Whole Grains (Quinoa, Brown Rice).
Zinc (Zn)
Immune Defense

Why it's vital: Essential for immune defense, DNA synthesis, cell division, and rapid wound healing.

Top Food Sources: Chickpeas (Chana), Pumpkin Seeds, Cashew Nuts, Sesame Seeds, Mushrooms, Yogurt, and Shellfish.
Potassium (K)
Heart & BP Control

Why it's vital: Offsets the negative effects of sodium to control blood pressure levels and maintain healthy heart rhythms.

Top Food Sources: Bananas, Coconut Water, Potatoes/Sweet Potatoes, Spinach, Oranges, and White Beans.
